# Alferes
Alferes | Title | A colonial Portuguese military rank, roughly equivalent to an ensign or junior officer. The most famous Alferes in Brazilian history was Joaquim José da Silva Xavier, better known as Tiradentes.

# Fifth
The Fifth | Taxes | A royal tax on gold mined in colonial Brazil. One-fifth (20%) of all extracted gold belonged to the Portuguese Crown.

# Derrama
Derrama | Taxes | A forced tax collection imposed when the required amount of gold was not delivered to the Crown. Officials could seize property and valuables to make up the difference. 

# paodequeijo
Pão de queijo | Foods | A small baked roll made from cassava starch and cheese. One of the most iconic foods of Minas Gerais. 

# vilarica
Vila Rica | Places | The colonial name of Ouro Preto, one of the richest mining cities in eighteenth-century Brazil and the center of the Inconfidência Mineira.

# clove
Clove | Foods | An aromatic spice used in cooking, medicine, and sweets during the colonial period. 

# Goiabada
Goiabada | Foods | A sweet preserve made from guavas and sugar, traditionally eaten with cheese in Brazil. 

# Horns
Horns | Sayings | "Chifre". A popular expression referring to someone whose spouse or partner has been unfaithful. 

# ball
Who owns the ball | Sayings | "O dono da bola". A modern Brazilian expression for someone who acts as if everything must go their way or believes they are in charge of everyone

# Captaincy
Captaincy | Places | A large administrative division of colonial Brazil, similar to a province or state.

# Truth
Naked and raw truth | Sayings | "Verdade Nua e Crua". The truth presented without embellishment or comforting lies. 

# Martyr
Martyr || A person who suffers or dies for their beliefs or principles. 

# Martyrdom
Martyrdom || Great suffering or death endured for one's beliefs, ideals, or cause. 

# Gallow
Gallow || A wooden platform used for public executions, particularly hangings and beheadings.
